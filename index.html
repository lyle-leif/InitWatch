    IIBrief description of the proposed solution

    One workflow from forecast to follow-up.

    InitWatch helps outdoor teams prepare for dangerous heat, reduce avoidable exposure, support gradual acclimatization, and make sure no worker is left unchecked.

    1. 1

      Warn ahead

      Hourly forecasts trigger alerts before heat turns dangerous.

    2. 2

      Precautions fitted to tasks, exposure and each worker's history.

    3. 3

    4. 4

      Notify a buddy

      Help requests and missed check-ins reach a person.

    5. 5

      Record follow-up

      Acknowledged, logged, and visible until resolved.

      VProposed key featuresHover a feature to see it on the phone

      Ten features, one workday.

      Location is captured only at the moment of a help request, with consent at registration. If it's unavailable, the registered worksite is sent instead. Shared locations are deleted at the end of the shift unless attached to an incident record.

      IIIGeneral objectives

      What we set out to build and prove.

      To develop and evaluate a localized heat-action system that helps outdoor work teams prepare for dangerous heat and reduce avoidable heat exposure through advance alerts, shift planning, guided acclimatization tracking, and timely buddy follow-up.

      Specific objectives
      • Present clear, location-based heat information with sources and update times.
      • Send forecast-based alerts ahead of high-heat periods, and start check-ins automatically at the Danger level.
      • Generate shift action plans from schedules, task intensity, exposure conditions and recent exposure history.
      • Track acclimatization with gradual exposure planning and supervisor review for new or returning workers.
      • Provide simple check-ins and help requests with one-time location sharing, linked to buddies and supervisors.
      • Give supervisors a dashboard of precautions, progress, unresolved notifications, follow-up and exportable records.
      • Deliver Filipino and English safety guidance, with selected content available offline.
      • Evaluate usability, understanding, completed precautions, and alert and follow-up performance in a small pilot and controlled scenarios.
      IVTarget users or beneficiaries

      Built for small outdoor teams that look out for each other.

      Workers

      Get shift guidance and alerts, answer check-ins, and request help without lengthy forms.

      Designated buddies

      Check on a coworker, respond to notifications, and record what they did.

      Foremen and supervisors

      Organize shifts, review acclimatization, and coordinate protective measures.

      Primary beneficiaries
      • Campus grounds and maintenance personnel
      • Construction crews
      • Agricultural work groups
      Pilot scope

      One outdoor work team in one locality, recruited during development. One complete worker–buddy–supervisor workflow.

      Future beneficiaries

      Students, commuters, drivers, and solo outdoor workers such as delivery riders and vendors, after the worker prototype is evaluated.

      VIProposed technologies or tools

      Simple, open tools. No custom hardware.

      Weather in

      Open-Meteo

      Hourly temperature and humidity → documented heat-index calculation.

      PAGASA

      Heat index categories, advisories and iHeatMap as official reference. Sources always labeled.

      Core

      Node.js + PostgreSQL

      Team profiles, shift plans, exposure logs, check-ins, incidents, follow-ups.

      Scheduled server tasks

      Advance alerts, check-ins and overdue notifications.

      Rule-based guidance

      Transparent rules from published heat-prevention resources, plus supervisor review.

      People out

      React + TypeScript PWA

      Mobile-first, in the browser, with offline guidance.

      Telegram bot

      For opted-in pilot users. Facebook Messenger after Meta's app review.

      Browser geolocation

      One-time pin, only on a help request, with map links.

      Later extensions:SMS (needs a funded channel)More user modesWearable integration
      VIDevelopment and evaluation scope

      How we'll know it works.

      A small pilot with one outdoor team in one locality, plus controlled scenarios that test what happens when things go wrong.

      We will measure
      • Alert timing
      • Completion of planned precautions and exposure logs
      • Understanding of guidance
      • Successful assistance routing and time to acknowledgment
      We will break it on purpose
      • Missed responses
      • Connectivity failures
      • Unresolved cases that must stay visible

      The prototype needs no custom hardware and focuses on one complete worker–buddy–supervisor workflow. SMS delivery, more user modes and wearables are later extensions.

      Alignment with the themeBuild the Way Forward

      Building the way forward, one safer workday at a time.

      Protective
      Action before harm

      Warns supervisors the evening before, starts check-ins automatically at the Danger level, and makes sure a person checks on any worker who stops responding.

      Inclusive
      Made for Filipino crews

      Guidance in Filipino and English, a safety guide that works offline, and a web app that runs on the phones workers already have. New and returning workers get extra care while they adjust.

      Feasible
      Ready to build and pilot

      Free and open tools, no custom hardware, and a scope of one team in one locality. It supports DOLE's heat advisories with exportable incident records.
